what this site collects

very little. there are no cookies, no advertising, no tracking across sites and no third-party embeds. fonts and the site's own scripts are served from flt.pt itself. the one exception is cloudflare web analytics, which counts visits (see analytics, below).

when you write to us

the brief form does not send anything: it opens your own email client with a message ready to go. if no mail client opens, a "copy the brief" button puts the same text on your clipboard so you can paste it into an email yourself; the clipboard is handled by your browser and nothing is sent or stored. what reaches us is the email you choose to send to info@flt.pt or to one of us directly, usually with your name, email address, company and the text of your message.

we use it only to reply and to discuss the work you ask about. the legal basis is taking steps at your request before a possible contract (article 6(1)(b) gdpr) and our legitimate interest in answering the people who write to us (article 6(1)(f) gdpr).

we keep it while the conversation is active and for up to 12 months after the last contact if no project follows. if we work together, it becomes part of the client record and is kept for the length of the contract and for the periods the law sets for accounting and tax records.

our email is hosted by google (google workspace), which processes it on our behalf.

hosting

the site is hosted on github pages. like any web server, github receives your ip address and the details of each request when you load a page, and may keep them in logs to run and secure the service, under github's privacy statement.

analytics

we use cloudflare web analytics to know how many people visit, which pages they read, where they come from and how fast the pages load. when you load a page, a small script from cloudflare sends it the page address, the site that sent you, your browser and device type, the country inferred from your ip address and page load timings. it sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and does not build a profile of you or follow you to other sites. we only see totals.

cloudflare, inc. processes this on our behalf. the legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used and keeping it fast (article 6(1)(f) gdpr). cloudflare may process data in the united states and is certified under the eu-us data privacy framework; more in cloudflare's privacy policy. you can object at any time by blocking the script in your browser (the site works the same without it) or by writing to privacy@flt.pt.

cookies and language

this site sets no cookies. if you use the pt / en switch, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (as fp-lang) so the site remembers it next time. it never leaves your device, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

the site is in portuguese by default. on the portuguese home page, if you have not used the switch, the site reads your browser's language setting and shows the english version when portuguese is not among your languages. that setting is not stored or sent anywhere.

both are strictly necessary for features you use, so they need no consent (article 5 of lei n.º 41/2004).

your rights

you can ask us for access to your data, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its use, or for a copy in a portable format, by writing to privacy@flt.pt. we reply within one month.

if you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the comissão nacional de proteção de dados (cnpd).

changes

if this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
