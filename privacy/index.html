who is responsible

floating point solutions, lda. (nipc 519644816), rua emigrantes, 12, santa catarina, 3840-572 vagos, portugal, registered at the conservatória do registo comercial de coimbra, is the controller of the personal data described here. for anything about this policy or your data, write to info@flt.pt.

what this site collects

nothing, on its own. there are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising or tracking, and no third-party embeds. fonts and scripts are served from flt.pt itself.

when you write to us

the brief form does not send anything: it opens your own email client with a message ready to go. what reaches us is the email you choose to send to info@flt.pt or to one of us directly, usually with your name, email address, company and the text of your message.

we use it only to reply and to discuss the work you ask about. the legal basis is taking steps at your request before a possible contract (article 6(1)(b) gdpr) and our legitimate interest in answering the people who write to us (article 6(1)(f) gdpr).

we keep it while the conversation is active and for up to 12 months after the last contact if no project follows. if we work together, it becomes part of the client record and is kept for the length of the contract and for the periods the law sets for accounting and tax records.

our email is hosted by google (google workspace), which processes it on our behalf.

hosting

the site is hosted on github pages. like any web server, github receives your ip address and the details of each request when you load a page, and may keep them in logs to run and secure the service, under github's privacy statement.

cookies and language

this site sets no cookies. if you use the pt / en switch, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (as fp-lang) so the site remembers it next time. it never leaves your device, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

on your first visit, the site reads your browser's language setting to show the portuguese version to portuguese readers. that setting is not stored or sent anywhere.

both are strictly necessary for features you use, so they need no consent (article 5 of lei n.º 41/2004).

your rights

you can ask us for access to your data, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its use, or for a copy in a portable format, by writing to info@flt.pt. we reply within one month.

if you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the comissão nacional de proteção de dados (cnpd).

changes

if this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
